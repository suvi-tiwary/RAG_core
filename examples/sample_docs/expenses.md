# Expenses policy

Submit expense claims within 30 days, with a photo of every receipt. Claims
are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Travel

Book train travel in second class. Flights are only allowed for trips longer
than six hours by train. Hotel costs are refunded up to 150 euros per night.

## Home office

Remote workers can claim up to 300 euros once for a desk and chair for their
home office. Internet costs are not refunded.
