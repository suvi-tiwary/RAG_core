# Remote work policy

Employees may work from home up to three days per week, after agreeing the
days with their manager. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 on every working day.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset for remote work.
Equipment stays company property and must be returned when you leave.

## Working abroad

Working from another country is allowed for at most 20 working days per year,
and needs written approval from HR at least two weeks in advance.
