# Leave policy

Every full-time employee gets 20 paid leave days per calendar year. Part-time
employees get leave days in proportion to their working hours.

Unused leave days can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of
5 days. Carried-over days must be used before the end of March.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from paid leave. Tell your manager before 10:00 on the
first day you are sick. From the third sick day you need a doctor's note.

## Parental leave

Parents get 16 weeks of paid parental leave after the birth or adoption of a
child. It can be split into at most three blocks within the first year.
